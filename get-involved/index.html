Work With Mitraja

Build alongside communities

Mitraja brings together people who believe that sustainable change begins with communities themselves. If you are interested in working directly with communities, supporting programs or bringing your professional skills to our work, there may be a place for you.

We welcome people with experience in areas such as:

Community Development · Youth Development · Education · Livelihoods · Research · Communication · Design · Program Management · Fundraising · Technology

Full-Time Roles

Join the Mitraja team and contribute to our long-term work.

Internships & Fellowships

Learn through meaningful engagement with our programs and communities.

Project-Based Opportunities

Bring specific expertise to a program, workshop or initiative.

Community-Based Roles

Work directly with young people and community members as facilitators, resource people or mentors.

Volunteer

Your time can become a community resource

Volunteering with Mitraja is not about coming in with answers. It is about listening, sharing, learning and contributing. Depending on your skills and interests, you could support:

Youth Workshops · Library Activities · Creative Livelihood Workshops · Sports & Community Activities · Communication & Storytelling · Documentation & Research · Events & Camps · Digital & Technology Initiatives · Fundraising & Outreach

You can contribute remotely or, where appropriate, directly with our community initiatives.

Donate

Help communities build what lasts

Your contribution can help create the conditions for communities to learn, participate, organise and lead. Your support can contribute towards:

Community Learning

Books, learning resources and community spaces.

Youth Development

Workshops, leadership development, sports and learning opportunities.

Creative Livelihoods

Skill development in pottery, bamboo craft, embroidery and other creative practices.

Community Activities

Camps, workshops and locally driven initiatives.

Community Infrastructure

Spaces and resources that communities can build, use and eventually manage themselves.

A community that begins to believe: we can build this ourselves.

Become an Ally

A friend stands beside you

Mitraja's name comes from the idea of friendship. And friendship shapes how we think about support. An ally doesn't arrive with all the answers. An ally listens. An ally connects. An ally contributes. An ally creates space.

You can become an ally by:

Sharing knowledge

Offer your expertise, mentorship, training or professional skills.

Sharing resources

Contribute books, materials, equipment, technology or financial resources.

Opening doors

Connect Mitraja or community initiatives to people, organisations and opportunities.

Amplifying stories

Help community stories, knowledge and achievements reach wider audiences.

Showing up

Follow our work, participate in events and stay connected.

You don't need a large organisation or a large contribution to become an ally. Sometimes, the most useful thing you can bring is a willingness to listen.

One Community. Many Ways to Join.

Everyone has something to contribute.

You may have knowledge. A skill. A network. Time. Books. Ideas. Or simply the willingness to listen.

A friend does not take over. A friend stands beside you.

That is the spirit we want to bring to community work.
