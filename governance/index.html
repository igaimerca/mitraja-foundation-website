Legal Structure Our Team

Legal Structure

Registered as a Section 8 Company under India's Companies Act, 2013, a non-profit structure built for organisations whose purpose is social, not commercial. Headquartered in Dudhnoi, Goalpara, Assam.

Community-Centered Leadership

We treat the whole community as our Mitras. Decisions on programs like Summer Camp are shaped with community strength-mapping and local resource people before, not instead of, board direction.
