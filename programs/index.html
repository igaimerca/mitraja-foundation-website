Summer Camp 2026 — seven days, six skills

13–19 July 2026, Sidhabari. Organised through Apollo Club to bring back school dropouts and inactive members, mapped local leadership and resource people, and ran on community trust rather than outside funding.

01Art & Creativity

Turning leaves and old newspapers into art

Facilitated by Subraato Dalu, a school teacher from the community. Participants explored creativity using naturally available materials, giving the Mitraja team a first read on imagination, confidence and creative interest across the group.

👥 26 participants🎨 Facilitator: Subraato Dalu
02Health & Hygiene

Yoga, nutrition and daily wellbeing

Warm-up exercises, nutrition, healthy food habits, dietary needs and basic hygiene — showing how locally available food already meets most nutritional needs.

👥 20 participants🧘 Gautam Dalu (Yoga & Fitness)🥗 Sukanta Bhowmick (Nutrition & Hygiene)
03Library

The joy of reading, made interactive

Quizzes, book categories and library-based games turned the community library into a shared learning space — and surfaced real curiosity in the children who came.

👥 16 participants📚 Suman Biswas & Arjun, trained with Free Library Network
04Pottery

Goalparia pottery, hands in the clay

Our biggest single-day turnout — participants were introduced to the rich tradition of Goalparia pottery through hands-on clay work, and to pottery as a real, creative livelihood.

👥 55 participants🏺 Banamallika Choudhary, Founder, Mudhill Pottery⭐ Guest: Sisir Basumatary
05Green Skills

Local flora, medicinal plants, and leadership

Local flora, medicinal plants, herbs and traditional plantation techniques, paired with reflection on community-based leadership and local ownership — this session is what sparked our plan to map medicinal plants in the local language.

👥 36 participants🌿 Jugantar Hazong & Nitai Sarkar🧑‍🌾 Leadership: Maheswar Hazong
06Sports

The day that united the community

Enthusiasm, unity, teamwork, and interaction across every age group — sports proved to be one of the strongest mediums for community mobilisation all week.

👥 73 participants⚽ Plabon Hazong & Dalim Dalu⭐ Guests: SELCO Kokrajhar team
07Closing Ceremony

Dance, music, and a community lunch

Songs, prize distribution and celebration closed the week — recognising every participant's contribution and cementing community ownership of what had just been built together.

👥 66 participants⭐ Guest: Benjamin Kamak, Krishi Vigyan Kendra, Dudhnoi
Community Ownership

Funded by the community, for the community

The Summer Camp was completed without external financial support. Small voluntary contributions from Mitraja members and the local community covered every expense. Local resource persons facilitated for free — some even contributed money themselves.

This is one of our strongest learnings, and it will guide how we plan every future program: start with what a community already has — trust, volunteer effort, and shared ownership — before reaching for outside funding.

🙌Community-funded, community-run
Learning Out Loud

What we're carrying into the next program

We keep our reflections visible on purpose — a foundation that only shows its wins isn't being honest about how community work actually gets built.

Challenges we saw

  • • Limited women's participation in some activities — only 2 women joined two of the sessions.
  • • Publicity and information about the camp didn't reach the whole community.
  • • Fundraising lacked a clear plan going in — we need a budget before the next one starts.

What we're changing

  • • Define facilitator roles clearly, with a registration process ahead of each session.
  • • Assign a real-time note-taker to capture participant reflections — missing this time.
  • • Map community strength and resource people before, not during, a program.
  • • Map and document local & medicinal plants in the local language — our next Green Skills step.
