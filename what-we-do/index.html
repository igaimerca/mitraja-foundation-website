Flagship Program

The Apollo Program

The Apollo Program transforms rural youth clubs into community-owned learning and resource hubs. It empowers youth to build confidence, create opportunities, and become changemakers within their own communities — by connecting education, entrepreneurship, local knowledge and community action.

🧠

Holistic Human Development Education

Life skills — self-awareness, communication, leadership, critical thinking, problem-solving, financial literacy, entrepreneurship and social-emotional learning.

🌿

Creative Skills for Green Livelihoods

Traditional arts, crafts and community knowledge blended with market trends — culturally rooted, environmentally responsible, economically viable.

🏠

Co-Designed Mitra Spaces

Community spaces turned into inclusive, youth-designed hubs for connection, learning and local opportunity.

Learning Hub

Apollo Library

Built from bamboo and donated books by community volunteers, in collaboration with the Free Library Network — a community learning hub promoting a culture of reading rather than a silent reading room, with interactive activities, quizzes and book-based games. Two community volunteers travelled to a Free Library Network seminar in Guwahati — their first night away from the village — and came back to take ownership of library activities, unprompted.
