A Real Story

A week that funded itself

🏺Day 4 — Pottery, 55 participants
"

Our July 2026 Summer Camp ran seven days — Art, Health & Hygiene, Library, Pottery, Green Skills, Sports and a Closing Ceremony — without a single rupee of outside funding. Local teachers, artists and knowledge-keepers facilitated for free; some even chipped in their own money. Attendance grew from 25 on Day 1 to 80 by the closing ceremony.

It told us something we'll build every future program around: this community already has the resource people, the trust, and the will. Our job is to make room for it.

— From the Summer Camp Completion Report, July 2026
