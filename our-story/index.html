Our Story

Nurturing potential,

Mitra means friend. Ja, origin. Mitraja Foundation is built on the conviction that friendship, scaled up with patience, can become a force for community transformation. Not delivered from outside, but grown from what a community already has.

Origin

From a question, to a circle, to an organisation

The question

"Friendship is the only cement that will ever hold the world together" (Woodrow Wilson) sat with a small circle of Mitras long enough to become a real question: if friendship helps us overcome personal struggles, can it also serve communities in need?

September 2025: registering the intent

Mitraja Foundation was registered as a Section 8 Company under India's Companies Act, 2013, based in Dudhnoi, Goalpara, Assam. It's led by Jugantar Sarma, a Fellow of the Youth Involve Node Fellowship, working alongside a co-founder from the local community.

A club named after a moon landing

Our entry point was the Apollo Club in Sidhabari, a community youth space that takes its name from the Apollo 11 moon landing of 1969. News of it once reached the village by radio and became a small, hopeful moment the community carried in its memory for decades. By 2025 the club had sat dormant for years, used only as a storeroom.

4 January 2026: reopening, by hand

Patient conversations beginning in October 2025 led to a collective decision to bring the club back to life. It reopened on 4 January 2026, with the community restoring the old bamboo structure through voluntary labour. Our role throughout was holding the community's hand, asking the right questions rather than directing the outcome.

Learning in public: Summer Camp 2026

A seven-day Summer Camp (13–19 July 2026) across Art, Health & Hygiene, Library, Pottery, Green Skills and Sports became our clearest proof of concept yet. Proposed by the community itself, facilitated almost entirely by local resource people, and run without a rupee of outside funding. Read the full story →

Our Vision

Communities that flourish alongside nature, led by their own young people.

We want marginalised communities empowered through transformative education that builds self-awareness, leadership and creativity, so young people can claim rights, access opportunity, and build careers through social enterprise, in step with community-led, climate-resilient development.

Our Mission

Transformative learning experiences beyond the classroom.

We combine education, skills, creativity and practice to equip young people to lead change and build meaningful futures: reviving community intelligence, promoting gender equality, and strengthening sustainable, local livelihoods.

Values, in Color

Every color on this site means something

We picked our palette the way we picked our programs, rooted in what's already here: Goalparia clay, green fields, and the quiet trust a library and a youth club need to work.

Mitra: Friendship

Marigold. The warmth that opens a door first. It marks every "join us" moment on this site, because that's what friendship does first: it invites.

Rootedness

Terracotta. The color of Goalparia clay and the pottery tradition we work with. A reminder that our programs grow out of what a community already has, not what we import.

Growth & Sustainability

Deep moss green. Green skills, medicinal plants, plantation work, and a climate-resilient future: the color of the land our work depends on.

Trust & Leadership

Indigo ink. The quiet anchor color, for the library, for governance, for the steady kind of trust that lets a community hand a stranger their children for an afternoon.
